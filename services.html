Services

Bridging the gap between raw numbers, enterprise software, and actionable leadership decisions.

Software Implementation & Optimization

Over a decade of experience in data integration means I ensure your software is configured with the right data and workflows from day one, so it supports your goals long after launch.

For healthcare clients, that includes deep hands-on experience with Cerner, Strata, Infor, and Epic—helping finance and operations teams work from a reliable, reconciled source of truth.

Project & Vendor Management

Technology rollouts and ERP migrations often stall when technical teams, operational leaders, and external vendors speak different languages.

I step in to coordinate vendors, manage project milestones, and follow through on root-cause troubleshooting so nothing falls through the cracks.

Data Analysis & Executive Reporting

Turning raw operational information into clear, actionable insights using SQL, Excel, Python, and modern data visualization tools.

For healthcare organizations, this includes building and visualizing profit and loss reports (such as service line reporting and capital request evaluations) to help leadership understand how they can best impact their bottom line.
