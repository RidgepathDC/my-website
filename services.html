Healthcare Services

Bridging clinical systems, ERP data, and healthcare finance to deliver reliable numbers and on-schedule implementations.

Healthcare Financial Analytics & Decision Support

Healthcare finance teams often have the right software in place, but lack the internal bandwidth to reconcile complex clinical and general ledger feeds. I provide hands-on financial modeling and decision support across core hospital workflows:

✓ Cost Accounting: Building, validating, and maintaining accurate cost models, RVU mappings, and overhead allocations so leadership can trust unit-level cost data.
✓ Service Line Assessments: Evaluating contribution margin, payer mix, and volume trends across clinical service lines to guide strategic growth and capital allocation.
✓ Multi-Year Forecasting: Developing dynamic long-range financial models that account for volume shifts, reimbursement changes, and capital initiatives.
✓ Community Care Report Preparation: Extracting, reconciling, and compiling uncompensated care and community benefit data accurately ahead of regulatory deadlines.

Implementation Project Management & Vendor Integration

Healthcare software implementations frequently stall in the gap between IT teams who know the database tables, finance teams who know the accounting rules, and vendors who only know their own platform.

With over a decade of healthcare data integration experience across Strata, Epic, Cerner, Infor, and Lawson, I serve as the technical translator and project manager. I coordinate vendors, validate source-to-target data mappings, and resolve bottlenecks early so implementations launch cleanly and stay on schedule.

Data Analysis & Executive Dashboards

You don't need to buy new software to get clearer visibility into performance. I work directly inside your hospital's existing analytics stack using SQL, Epic Reporting, Power BI, Tableau, and Excel to transform raw clinical and financial extracts into intuitive executive dashboards.

From automated service line P&L views to capital request evaluations and operational KPI tracking, I build reporting tools that give leaders clear, immediate visibility into how they can impact the bottom line.

Flexible Engagement Models

Every service above is available in the format that best fits your team's current bandwidth and budget:

✓ Project-Based Consulting: Scoped, end-to-end delivery with clear milestones for system implementations, cost model builds, multi-year forecasts, and regulatory reporting.
✓ Staff Augmentation & Interim Support: Embedded, senior-level capacity to cover decision support or analytics vacancies, parental leaves, budget-season surges, or system go-lives with minimal ramp-up time.
