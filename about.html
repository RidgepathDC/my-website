About Ridgepath

Helping community hospitals, health systems, and specialty clinics turn complex clinical and financial data into clean analytics, accurate cost models, and on-time implementations.

Healthcare technology should support clinical and financial clarity rather than create extra administrative friction. Yet even top-tier enterprise systems often fall short when clinical data, general ledger feeds, and operational workflows aren't properly aligned.

That’s where I come in. With over a decade of experience in enterprise data and analytics across ERP, banking, and high-volume transactional environments (including four-plus years dedicated to healthcare technology and prior vendor-side experience as a Technical Consultant at Strata Decision Technology), I work alongside hospital finance, decision support, and IT teams to bridge the gap between raw system data and executive decision-making.

Because I understand both the underlying database structures and the healthcare finance rules behind them, I can step into complex environments (whether an independent community hospital, a specialty clinic, or a multi-hospital health system) and keep implementations, data extracts, and reporting initiatives moving without a lengthy learning curve.

From standing up cost accounting and multi-year forecasting models to validating data specifications and managing ongoing vendor relations across Strata, Epic, Cerner, Infor, GHR, and Lawson, my goal is straightforward: give your team clean, trustworthy numbers and the tools to act on them.

Direct, Dedicated Execution

Engagements at Ridgepath are direct. As a solo practitioner, the person scoping your project timeline is the exact same person writing extracts or partnering with your IT team, validating specs for downstream accuracy, reconciling the numbers, and coordinating with your vendors.

You get transparent communication, zero handoffs to junior staff, and dedicated follow-through from kickoff to ongoing support.

✓ EHR & ERP Data Extracts & Validation (Epic, Cerner, Infor, GHR, Lawson)
✓ Decision Support & Cost Accounting (Strata)
✓ Executive Analytics (SQL, Power BI, Tableau)
