About Ridgepath

Where healthcare finance, clinical data, and technical execution meet.

Healthcare technology should support clinical and financial clarity—not create extra administrative friction. Yet even top-tier enterprise systems often fall short when clinical data, general ledger feeds, and operational workflows aren't properly aligned.

That’s where I come in. With over a decade of experience in healthcare technology and financial analytics, I work alongside hospital finance, decision support, and IT teams to bridge the gap between raw system data and executive decision-making.

Because I understand both the underlying data structures and the healthcare finance rules behind them, I can step into complex environments—whether an independent community hospital, a specialty clinic, or a multi-hospital health system—and keep implementations and reporting initiatives moving without a lengthy learning curve.

From standing up cost accounting and multi-year forecasting models to managing vendor integrations across Strata, Epic, Cerner, Infor, and Lawson, my goal is straightforward: give your team clean, trustworthy numbers and the tools to act on them.

Direct, Dedicated Execution

Engagements at Ridgepath are direct. As a solo practitioner, the person scoping your data architecture and project timeline is the exact same person writing the queries, reconciling the numbers, and coordinating with your vendors.

You get transparent communication, zero handoffs to junior staff, and dedicated follow-through from kickoff to launch.

✓ Decision Support & Cost Accounting (Strata)
