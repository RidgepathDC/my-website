About Ridgepath

Focused healthcare financial decision support backed by vendor-side implementation depth and complex data systems experience.

Bridging Finance Strategy and Underlying Data

Hospital and health system finance teams often sit between two worlds: executive leadership needing reliable cost, margin, and forecast figures, and complex EHR/ERP source systems that require deep technical fluency to extract and reconcile.

Ridgepath Data Consulting was founded to close that gap. With specialized healthcare decision support experience—including vendor-side implementation work at Strata Decision Technology alongside broader enterprise data and ERP systems background—we bring both technical precision and practical finance perspective to every engagement.

Systems & Platforms

Strata Decision Technology, Epic, Cerner, Infor, GHR, and Lawson.

Analytics & Reporting Stack

SQL, Epic Reporting, Power BI, Tableau, and advanced financial modeling in Excel.
