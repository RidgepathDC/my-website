About Ridgepath

Bridging the gap between raw numbers and actionable decisions.

At Ridgepath Data Consulting, we specialize in helping organizations modernize their data architecture, design clean reporting systems, and streamline business analytics.

Whether you are converting legacy ERP databases, unifying disparate data sources, or building executive dashboards, we bring clarity to the noise.

✓ Data Integration & System Architecture
✓ Financial & Operational Analytics
✓ ERP Migration & Data Clean-up

Technology should work for you, not against you. But even the best tools can be challenging to set up, integrate, or maintain without the right support. That’s where I come in: I work alongside your team to achieve your goals, reduce friction, and make sure the end result is exactly what you envisioned, along with ongoing support to keep it running smoothly.

With a background in healthcare tech, start-ups, and broader business operations, I offer:

Software implementation & optimization – Over a decade of experience in data integration means I ensure your software is configured with the right data and workflows from day one, so it supports your goals long after launch. For healthcare clients, that includes deep experience with Cerner, Strata, Infor, and Epic.

Project & vendor management – Coordinating vendors, managing projects, and following through on troubleshooting so nothing falls through the cracks.

Data analysis – Turning raw information into actionable insights using SQL, Excel, Python, or data visualization tools. For healthcare clients this includes creating and visualizing profit and loss reports (service line reporting, capital request evaluations, etc) to better help your leaders understand how they can best impact their bottom line.

Whether you’re a growing business, a healthcare system, or a nonprofit organization, I partner with you to make technology work for you so your impact can grow.
